## What Is RHEO, Really?

Not the feature list. Not the tech stack. What does it MEAN?

RHEO is:
- **A relationship with your own time.** Most people don't know where their hours go. RHEO makes time visible — not as a spreadsheet, but as a living thing you can see, touch, and understand.
- **Privacy as a design principle, not a feature.** The app doesn't phone home because it CAN'T. Your data lives in one SQLite file on your machine. That's not a spec — it's a statement about who owns your life.
- **AI that works FOR you, not ON you.** The AI doesn't send your data to the cloud. It reads your local context and helps you locally. It's a tool you control, not a service that controls you.
- **Modularity as honesty.** The app has 15+ subsystems. It doesn't pretend to be one thing. It's a collection of tools that work together — like a workshop, not a black box.
- **The beauty of the process.** Tracking time, learning, budgeting — these aren't exciting on their own. But when you see the PATTERN, when the data becomes a story about your life — that's the beauty.

## What We Need From You

### 1. Which Story Are We Telling?

Pick ONE core narrative (or propose a better one):

**A. "The River of Time"**
- Time flows, data accumulates, AI organizes
- Visual: flowing particles, water, current
- Feeling: poetic, emotional, personal
- Risk: might feel too abstract

**B. "The Loom"**
- Threads = subsystems, shuttle = AI, fabric = your life
- Visual: woven threads, interlocking pattern
- Feeling: craft, precision, interconnectedness
- Risk: might feel too mechanical

**C. "The Living System"**
- The app breathes, grows, adapts to you
- Visual: organic patterns, reaction-diffusion, neural networks
- Feeling: alive, personal, evolving
- Risk: might feel too sci-fi

**D. "The Workshop"**
- Build your own setup, pick your tools
- Visual: modular components, assembly, construction
- Feeling: practical, empowering, hands-on
- Risk: might feel too generic

**E. Something else entirely.**

- Something else?
