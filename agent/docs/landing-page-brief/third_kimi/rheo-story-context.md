# RHEO — The Story (for External AI)

> This is NOT a feature list. This is what RHEO means.

---

## The Problem RHEO Solves

Most people have no idea where their time goes. They use 10 different apps — a timer here, a budget there, a note-taking app, a learning platform, an AI chatbot. None of them talk to each other. None of them see the full picture.

And all of them send your data to someone else's server.

RHEO exists because:
- **Your time is precious.** You should be able to see exactly where it goes.
- **Your data is yours.** It shouldn't live on someone else's server.
- **AI should work for you.** Not mine your data. Not train on your conversations. Just help you, locally, privately.
- **Tools should connect.** Your learning should inform your focus sessions. Your finance should connect to your goals. Your life phases should be visible as a timeline.

---

## What RHEO Actually Is

A desktop app. Electron + React + SQLite. One file on your machine. No account required. No cloud. No subscription.

### It Tracks
Every app you use, every website you visit, every minute of focus. Not to judge you — to help you see patterns.

### It Learns
A hierarchical lesson system (Lyceum) where AI generates lessons from your knowledge base. Mastery levels L0-L5. Visual grounding. Annotated code blocks.

### It Thinks
A bitemporal knowledge graph (Context Brain) that remembers everything. Episodes, entities, facts, embeddings. Your second brain, powered by AI — but running locally.

### It Connects
15+ subsystems that talk to each other. Your tracked time feeds into your focus goals. Your learning progress informs your AI chat. Your life phases connect to your reflections.

### It Protects
Zero telemetry. Zero tracking. The app can't phone home because there's nowhere to phone TO. Your data is in one SQLite file. If we disappear tomorrow, your data lives on.

## The Users

RHEO is for people who:
- Care about where their time goes
- Want AI help without giving up their data
- Prefer local tools over cloud services
- Build things (developers, creators, learners)
- Value privacy as a principle, not a checkbox

## The Visual Identity

RHEO's app uses:
- Dark mode only (zinc-950 background)
- Amber gold accent (#fbbf24)
- Glass morphism cards
- Geist font (body) + JetBrains Mono (code/labels)
- Reaction-diffusion ambient background (Living Substrate)
- Per-page accent colors (emerald for dashboard, indigo for IDE, amber for life, violet for AI)

The landing page should feel like an extension of the app — not a separate marketing site.
