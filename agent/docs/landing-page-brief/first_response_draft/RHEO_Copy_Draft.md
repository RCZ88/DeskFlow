## SECTION 2: THE CURRENT (Philosophy)

### Kicker
PHILOSOPHY

### Headline
Your data never leaves your machine.

### Subhead
No accounts. No cloud. No subscription. Everything lives in a single SQLite file on your computer.

### Pillars

**Local**
One file. Your entire life.
Everything — every tracked minute, every lesson, every transaction — lives in a single `deskflow-data.db` on your machine. No sync servers. No dependencies.

**Private**
Zero telemetry. Zero tracking.
We don't know you exist. No analytics, no crash reports, no usage data leaves your computer. Your life is yours alone.

**Yours**
Open source. Extensible. Forever.
RHEO is MIT licensed. Your data is standard SQLite. If we disappear tomorrow, your data and your workflow live on.

## SECTION 3: THE TRIBUTARIES (Features)

### Kicker
FEATURES

### Headline
Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

### Subhead
15+ subsystems that talk to each other. Track time, learn with AI, manage money, and visualize your life — all in one place.

### Feature Cards

**AI Assistant**
Multi-provider chat with vision and memory. OpenRouter, Ollama local, or direct API. Your AI knows your context because it lives where your data lives.

**Content Studio**
Brainstorm → ideas → scripts → publish. A full content creation pipeline with retention evidence, SEO scoring, and gate validation.

**Focus Sessions**
Strict timer with app blocking and browser tab tracking. Whitelist allowed apps, set daily goals, build streaks. Your productive time, protected.

**Lyceum Learn**
Hierarchical lessons: Topics → Groups → Nodes. Mastery levels L0-L5, prerequisites, visual grounding, and AI-powered quiz generation.

**Gold & Finance**
Wallet management, income/expense tracking, subscriptions, and spending analytics. Progress rings, deadline radar, and monthly recaps.

**River of Years**
Visual timeline of your life phases. Ring & grain visualization, era trends, reflections, and covenant completion tracking.

**Terminal Workspace**
Multi-pane terminal with AI agent integration. 5-group sidebar, presets, configs, file browser, and session management.

**Context Brain**
Bitemporal knowledge graph. Episodes, entities, facts, embeddings. Keyword + graph retrieval. MCP server for external tools.
