# RHEO Landing Page Design — Creative Brief for External AI Designer

You are a world-class landing page designer. I need you to generate 3-5 completely different, creative, meaningful design directions for a landing page. Each direction must have a strong visual metaphor with intention behind it — not just "looks cool."

---

## WHAT IS RHEO?

RHEO is a **local-first, privacy-first desktop application** (Electron + React + SQLite). It's a multi-functional productivity and life-management system with 15+ subsystems.

**The one-sentence pitch:** "Your data never leaves your machine. No accounts. No cloud. No subscription. One app that tracks, learns, manages money, and runs AI — all locally."

**Core philosophy:** Everything lives in a single SQLite file on your computer. Zero telemetry. Zero tracking. Open source (MIT).

**What it does:**
- Tracks your app usage, browser tabs, coding time, focus sessions
- AI chat with multiple providers (OpenRouter, Ollama local, direct API)
- Hierarchical learning system with AI-powered lessons and quizzes
- Finance tracking (wallets, expenses, subscriptions, spending analytics)
- Life timeline visualization (phases, goals, reflections)
- Terminal workspace with AI agents
- Knowledge graph (Context Brain) that remembers everything
- Content creation pipeline (brainstorm → script → publish)

**The app feels like:** A personal command center. A private dashboard. A second brain that lives on your machine.

**Target audience:** Power users, developers, privacy-conscious people, quantified-self enthusiasts, people tired of 15 different subscriptions.

---

## WHAT THE LANDING PAGE MUST COMMUNICATE (in order of importance)

1. **"Your data never leaves your machine"** — THE differentiator. Privacy-first, local-first.
2. **"It's not one app — it's 15+ tools that work together"** — Multi-functional, modular.
3. **"AI-native, not AI-bolted-on"** — AI is woven into every feature.
4. **"Track everything, see everything, improve everything"** — The beauty of the process.
5. **"Free and open source"** — No subscription, no lock-in.

---

## CRITICAL CONSTRAINT

**DO NOT use any existing app visualizations on the landing page.** The app already has:
- Solar system visualization
- City visualization  
- 3D architecture maps
- Life phases ring & grain visualization
- Reaction-diffusion patterns
- Force-directed graphs

**The landing page needs its OWN unique visual identity.** It should be the invitation to the app, not a preview of what's inside. Think of it like the difference between a movie poster and the movie itself.

## THE STORE CONCEPT (one section of the page)

The landing page includes a playful "module store" section:
- Individual features displayed as purchasable modules: $2.99 each
- Bundles (3 features): $6.99 each
- Admin toggle unlocks everything (flips prices to "Included")
- This is a visual metaphor for modularity, NOT real commerce

## YOUR TASK

Generate **3-5 completely different design directions**. Each must be:
- **Visually distinct** from the others (different color palettes, different metaphors, different moods)
- **Meaningful** — every visual choice must tie back to what RHEO is
- **Memorable** — someone should screenshot it and share it
- **Implementable** — use the tools listed above, no "magic" that doesn't exist

### For Each Candidate, Provide:

#### 1. Name & Metaphor
Give it a name and explain the core metaphor. Why does this metaphor fit RHEO?

#### 2. Mood & Tone
What does it feel like? (e.g., "mysterious and powerful," "clean and clinical," "warm and inviting")

#### 3. Color Scheme
- Background colors
- Text colors  
- Accent colors
- Why these colors?

#### 4. Hero Section
- What does the user see first?
- What's the visual hook? (animation, shader, illustration, typography treatment)
- Headline text
- Subheadline text
- CTA text
- Load animation sequence

#### 5. Full Section Flow (8 sections)
Map out what happens as the user scrolls. Name each section and describe its purpose.

#### 6. Key Animations (top 3)
- What animates?
- What library/technique?
- What does the animation communicate?

#### 7. Why This Fits RHEO
Explain the intention. Why is this the right design for THIS specific app?

## EXAMPLE TO SHOW DEPTH

### Example: "The Vault"
**Metaphor:** RHEO is a secure vault for your life data.
**Mood:** Heavy, impenetrable, precious.
**Colors:** Gunmetal gray, steel silver, amber warning lights, deep black.
**Hero:** A massive vault door engraved with "RHEO." As you scroll, the door slowly rotates open (3D transform), gears turn, locks click. Behind it: a glimpse of the app's dark interface. Amber warning lights pulse.
**Animation:** Vault door rotation on scroll, gear mechanics, amber pulse lights, blur reveal of app behind door.
**Why:** Makes "local-first" feel tangible. Your data is precious enough to vault.

---

## RULES

1. **No existing app visualizations** — solar system, city, 3D maps, etc. are OFF LIMITS
2. **Every animation needs a reason** — not decoration, communication
3. **One focal element per viewport** — don't clutter
4. **Typography hierarchy** — use scale and weight, not borders and pills
5. **Dark mode only** — this is a privacy app, not a SaaS dashboard
6. **Mobile dignity** — it should work on phone, not just "shrunk desktop"
7. **Reduced motion respect** — must work without animations for accessibility
