# Lyceum Welcome Illustration Prompt

## Style Reference (Ian Xiaohei / 小黑 Style)
- Pure white background, no texture, no beige, no shadows, no gradients
- Black hand-drawn line art, thin lines, slight wobble/jitter
- Large white space, subject occupies only 40-60% of the frame
- Sparse red, orange, blue Chinese handwritten annotations
- One image expresses ONE core action, structure, state, or metaphor
- The character "小黑" (Xiaohei) must participate in the core action, not just decoration
- Whimsical, creative, refreshing, but NOT childish, NOT cute/kawaii

## Character: 小黑 (Xiaohei)
- Solid black body, white dot eyes, thin legs, expressionless face
- NOT a mascot, NOT a sticker, NOT standing in the corner
- An absurd worker seriously participating in the system

## Illustration Concepts for Learn Page Welcome

### 1. "Opening the First Book"
小黑 stands before a giant open book that towers over him. He's reaching up to turn the first page. The page glows slightly. Small red annotation: "第一章" (Chapter One). The book has visible chapter tabs on the side. Style: the book is drawn with slightly wobbly lines, the pages have subtle curl at the edges.

### 2. "Building Knowledge Shelves"
小黑 is on a wooden ladder, placing a book onto a high shelf. Below him, several shelves are already filled with books of different thicknesses. A few books are mid-air, floating toward their spots. Orange annotation: "知识上架" (Knowledge Shelving). The shelves have a warm wood grain texture drawn in simple lines.

### 3. "The Reading Path"
小黑 walks along a path made of open books laid flat on the ground, like stepping stones. Each book shows a different symbol (lightbulb, gear, atom, chart). The path leads toward a glowing doorway in the distance. Blue annotation: "学习之路" (Learning Path). The perspective is isometric, slightly tilted.

### 4. "Watering the Knowledge Tree"
小黑 holds a watering can, pouring water onto a small tree whose leaves are tiny open books. Some books are budding (small), some are fully grown (large). The tree trunk has growth rings visible. Red annotation: "成长" (Growth). A few droplets splash off the leaves.

### 5. "The Welcome Mat"
小黑 unrolls a red carpet that transforms into an open book as it unfurls. Behind him, a library doorway with warm light spilling out. The carpet/book hybrid has visible text lines on it. Orange annotation: "欢迎" (Welcome). Small sparkles around the doorway.

### 6. "Collecting Stars of Wisdom"
小黑 reaches up with a butterfly net, trying to catch floating star-shaped light bulbs. Some stars have already been caught in a jar beside him. The jar glows. Blue annotation: "智慧之星" (Stars of Wisdom). The stars leave trailing light paths.
